About the championship

Beauty Dynastya is an international online competition for beauty masters. Compete from your own studio, anywhere in the world — your work is evaluated by an international jury of leading industry professionals.

How it works

From registration to results — step by step.

  1. Registration

    Choose your category and nomination, complete the application and confirm your participation.

  2. Preparation

    Create your competition entry according to the rules of your nomination.

  3. Submission

    Upload a photo or video, check the quality and requirements — and submit on time.

  4. Jury evaluation

    International judges score every entry against approved criteria: technique, quality and aesthetics.

  5. Results

    Scores and placements are published in an official announcement.

  6. Awards

    Winners receive diplomas and awards that elevate their professional portfolio.
